Community Guidelines

HelpLoop aims to provide a welcoming space for people to connect, exchange ideas and support one another. These guidelines explain the behaviour expected from everyone using the platform.

By participating in HelpLoop, you agree to follow these guidelines along with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Treat Everyone with Respect

Be polite and considerate. Respect different opinions, backgrounds and experiences. Disagreement is acceptable; personal attacks are not.

2. Do Not Harass or Bully

Do not threaten, intimidate, target, insult or repeatedly contact people in an unwanted manner. Content intended to humiliate or harm others is not welcome.

3. Share Appropriate Content

Do not post unlawful, threatening, sexually exploitative, hateful or otherwise harmful material. Avoid content that promotes violence or encourages wrongdoing.

4. Protect Personal Information

Do not publish another person's private information without permission. Be careful when sharing your own contact details, location, financial information or other sensitive data.

5. Avoid Spam and Misleading Activity

Do not flood discussions, repeatedly post irrelevant promotions, impersonate others or deliberately share deceptive information. Keep contributions relevant and genuine.

6. Respect Intellectual Property

Only share material you have the right to use. Give credit where appropriate and do not copy or distribute someone else's protected work without permission.

7. Use Messages Responsibly

Keep private conversations respectful. Do not use messaging features for harassment, scams, unsolicited spam or threats.

8. Report Problems

If you encounter harmful behaviour or content, use the support options available on HelpLoop to report the issue. Provide clear and relevant details.

Enforcement

Where appropriate, HelpLoop may review reported activity and take action to protect the platform and its members. Actions may include removing content, restricting access or suspending accounts, depending on the circumstances and applicable policies.
