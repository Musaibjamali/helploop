Getting Started

How HelpLoop Works

HelpLoop brings community conversations, learning and support together in one online platform. Follow these steps to get started.

STEP 01

Create Your Account

Use the sign-up option on HelpLoop to create an account with your email address and the required details. Keep your login information secure.

STEP 02

Explore the Community

Visit the community area to read discussions, share relevant ideas and participate in conversations. Communicate respectfully with other members.

STEP 03

Connect Through Messages

Use the messaging features available on the platform to communicate with other members. Be thoughtful about the personal information you share.

STEP 04

Explore Learning and Support

Explore the education and help resources available in your account. You can also use the support area to seek assistance with platform-related questions.

STEP 05

Use Available Tools Responsibly

Explore tools such as the AI Assistant and notifications where available. Review important information and use these features responsibly.

Be a Positive Community Member

HelpLoop works best when members contribute useful information, respect different perspectives and follow the community guidelines.
