Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HelpLoop ("we", "our" or "us") may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit or use our website and services.

By using HelpLoop, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue use of the platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use HelpLoop, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Email address and other details you provide when creating or updating an account.
  • Profile information: Information you choose to add to your user profile.
  • Communications: Messages, community posts, support enquiries and other content you submit through the platform.
  • Usage information: Interactions with platform features, pages and services.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device details, IP address and diagnostic data where made available through hosting or service providers.

Information collected may vary depending on the features you use and the permissions you provide.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create, maintain and secure user accounts.
  • Provide community, messaging, education and support features.
  • Respond to enquiries and technical support requests.
  • Maintain platform functionality and improve user experience.
  • Detect abuse, spam, security incidents and violations of our policies.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Authentication and Data Storage

HelpLoop uses Supabase services for authentication and database functionality. Information needed to operate your account and platform features may be processed and stored through these services.

Such information is subject to the relevant service provider's infrastructure, security practices and privacy terms. We encourage users to review the provider's privacy information where relevant.

4. Messages and Community Content

Information you submit in community discussions, messages or support requests may be processed to provide the relevant features. Content shared with other members may be visible to those members according to the feature's design and settings.

Do not share passwords, financial credentials or sensitive personal information in public discussions or messages.

5. AI Assistant

If you use AI-powered features, the text or other information you submit may be processed to generate a response. Processing may involve third-party AI service providers, depending on the feature configuration.

Avoid submitting confidential, highly sensitive or unnecessary personal information to AI tools. AI-generated answers may be inaccurate and should not be treated as professional advice.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HelpLoop or its service providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies where needed for authentication, security, preferences and website functionality.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

HelpLoop may rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, databases, AI functionality and other technical operations. The platform may also contain links to external websites.

Third-party services and websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites.

8. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared only where reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate the platform through service providers.
  • Protect users, platform security and legitimate interests.
  • Respond to lawful requests or comply with legal obligations.
  • Address abuse, fraud or serious policy violations.

9. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and notifying us if they suspect unauthorized account access.

10. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain account records, resolve disputes, enforce policies or meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and purpose.

11. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request correction or deletion of information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

HelpLoop is not intended for children under the age required to independently use online services under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
